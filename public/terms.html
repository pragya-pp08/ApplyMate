Your responsibility

You must review every answer for truth and completeness, comply with each application site's rules, complete CAPTCHA and verification challenges yourself, and initiate every final submission.

Prohibited use

Do not use ApplyMate to fabricate qualifications, bypass security or CAPTCHA, scrape services without authorization, spam employers, impersonate another person, or submit information you are not authorized to provide.

Service limitations

Web forms change frequently. ApplyMate may fail to recognize a field or may suggest an incorrect match. The software therefore uses confidence thresholds and a mandatory review gate, but no automated system can guarantee error-free applications.

Third-party services

Google, Microsoft, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, job boards, and application portals operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Availability of an integration does not imply endorsement by those services.

Operator details

Before launch, add the service operator, governing law, warranty terms, liability terms, termination procedure, contact details, and any consumer notices required in the deployment jurisdiction.
