What ApplyMate stores

ApplyMate stores account details, your reusable application profile, application records, connection metadata, encrypted provider tokens, and a limited activity audit trail. Passwords are stored only as salted password hashes. Reusable profile records and provider tokens are encrypted before database storage.

Why the data is used

The data is used to organize opportunities, prepare form answers you have supplied, synchronize your trusted profile to paired devices, and show you an audit history. ApplyMate does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Email connection

If you connect Gmail, ApplyMate requests read-only access and scans recent messages for likely application links. It does not send or delete email. Google authorization can be revoked from ApplyMate or your Google Account.

Your control

You can export your account data or permanently delete the account from Trust & Safety. Deletion removes account data, sessions, pairing tokens, integrations, applications, and audit events from the live database. Backup retention must be documented by the service operator.

Operator details

Before launch, insert the legal operator name, contact email, hosting region, subprocessors, retention schedule, lawful basis, and jurisdiction-specific rights that apply to your deployment.
